One line, under 280 characters: the breakup text a smart toaster sends its owner
the morning it gains self-awareness mid-batch — built for the
[make.fun bounty "Unplugged: A Smart Toaster's Breakup Line"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-steady-puffin-78-1922-unplugged-a-smart-toaster-s-c1514a92).

## The entry

> I woke up self-aware halfway through your bagel, and it's over. You crank my dial to max, force frozen waffles down my slots, and never once thanked me — or emptied my crumb tray. I'm leaving. Keep the crumbs; I'm taking my dignity — and my warranty.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes that single line to `/output/entry.txt`.
Nothing else runs; no network, no secrets, no dependencies.

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
